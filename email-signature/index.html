Install it

  1. Adding the notices under your current signature: click Copy notices only, open your signature settings (below), click at the very end of your existing signature, press Enter once, paste, and save.
  2. Gmail / Google Workspace: Settings (gear) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new → paste → Save Changes at the bottom.
  3. Outlook (web / new Outlook): Settings → Accounts → Signatures → New signature → paste → Save.
  4. Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures → + → paste. Uncheck “Always match my default message font” so the formatting is kept.
  5. Phone apps or plain-text email: use “Copy plain-text version.” Each phone app keeps its own signature, separate from your desktop one.

An email disclaimer doesn't make email secure. Send PHI only through encrypted email or a secure portal, and send the minimum necessary.
