# AdvanceAPractice — Project Guidance

These are standing business and product decisions for **AdvanceAPractice Management** (AdvanceAPractice.com), a nationwide healthcare practice management, credentialing, revenue cycle, and operations firm. Keep public content accurate, professional, and focused on serving healthcare practices. Never invent clients, testimonials, logos, case studies, results, or partnerships.

## Client Welcome Announcements and Publicity Consent
**Decision recorded October 13, 2026. Status: approved strategic direction for future implementation, NOT yet integrated into live onboarding, agreements, or posting workflows.**

- Build an **optional, opt-in** opportunity for newly onboarded practice clients to be welcomed publicly on AdvanceAPractice's business social media. Prioritize LinkedIn; reuse on other approved channels when appropriate. The goal is legitimate B2B social proof and professional relationship-building, not promotional pressure.
- Never assume that a signed agreement, payment, BAA, business contact, or onboarding answer authorizes public identification. No default-on consent, implied endorsements, or automatic announcement.
- When future work involves onboarding, add a small **separate, optional publicity authorization** after the client is contracted, preferably within the existing lightweight authorization/e-sign workflow. Do not block onboarding if it is skipped or declined. Preserve the established upload-first, minimal-question onboarding model; do not silently add required fields or materially modify legal agreements.
- Request permission for each relevant item: the practice's **public-facing name**, **logo/trademark**, **general relationship**, **scope of services**, **social channels**, and optionally a tag to the client's official profile. Make clear that the client can decline without affecting service. Require an authorized practice representative's affirmative approval, and seek client approval of the actual copy/artwork before publication.
- Preserve a record of consent, its scope, any approved artwork/copy, approver, dates, channels, and revocation instructions. Suggested internal fields: publicity_permission (not_requested / declined / permission_granted / pending_post_approval / approved_for_post / published), consent_reference, approved_name, logo_allowed, channels, copy_approved_at, posted_urls. These are design suggestions, not a claim of existing implementation.
- Post **only after** the client relationship is executed and the practice has agreed to the specific announcement. Owner/operator review is required before anything goes live. Never auto-publish or imply the practice endorses AdvanceAPractice beyond the permission actually granted.
- **Never** include patient information, PHI, account or billing details, clinical outcomes, provider data that is not intentionally public, or confidential operational information. The client-practice name is usually business information rather than patient PHI, but client contract/confidentiality, trademark permission, and HIPAA boundaries still matter.
- A welcome post is not a client testimonial. Keep language factual: "We're pleased to welcome [approved practice name] as an AdvanceAPractice client. We look forward to supporting [approved general service category]." Do not claim results before they exist.
- If a client declines, publish an **anonymous** business milestone or general practice-growth content only if it cannot identify the client by inference; otherwise publish nothing about that client. Testimonials, case studies, and any results-based claims require a later, separate approval and substantiation.
- Keep human review and choice central; do not trade discounts or services for positive public statements without assessing FTC material-connection disclosure requirements.

For future marketing/product/Codex work, treat these points as a planned feature and drafting standard. Do not represent it as already deployed.
