# AdvanceAPractice — Project Guidance

These are standing business and product decisions for **AdvanceAPractice Management** (AdvanceAPractice.com), a nationwide healthcare practice management, credentialing, revenue cycle, and operations firm. Keep public content accurate, professional, and focused on serving healthcare practices. Never invent clients, testimonials, logos, case studies, results, or partnerships.

## Default-In Client Welcome Announcements and Publicity Controls

**Business decision updated October 13, 2026. Planned implementation: NOT yet deployed or activated in live onboarding, agreements, or posting workflows.**

**Existing contract baseline:** The AAP Contract Masters dated October 10, 2026, Section 10 (“Confidentiality & Publicity”), currently states: “AAP may identify the Client as a client in general marketing unless the Client opts out in writing.” Treat this as a draft-master finding, not proof every executed agreement contains it. Confirm the actual client contract and seek legal review before changing its scope.

- **Default-in policy, not a separate opt-in solicitation.** For new contracted practice clients, make factual professional welcome announcements a normal part of onboarding when a signed, applicable agreement actually authorizes them and the client has not opted out. Lead with LinkedIn, and consider AAP's other business social channels. Do not presume permission from a BAA alone, a prospect record, unsigned paperwork, or client silence when an applicable signed contract does not authorize publicity.
- **Acknowledge, do not upsell.** Present a brief, conspicuous onboarding notice describing the standard announcement. Include an un-prechecked, required **acknowledgment of having read the notice** (not a coercive consent checkbox). The acknowledgment is separate from a genuine option to exclude the practice. Include the practice name and who made the acknowledgment in the audit trail.
- **Written opt-out is always available.** Client may decline through a clearly labeled simple written request field or by email (info@advanceapractice.com). A short free-text statement such as “Please do not announce our practice” is sufficient. Do not hide this option, require exact magic words, force extra screens, or otherwise intentionally make opting out difficult. Declining must not affect services. Record and honor requests promptly, including requests received after signing or posting.
- **Scope is limited and explicit.** Existing draft contract expressly addresses identifying the practice as a client in general marketing; it does **not by itself establish unlimited permission** to use logos, quote a provider, tag accounts, publish nonpublic service details, or claim endorsement. Before standardized branded welcome graphics, add a properly reviewed, narrow authorization for practice logo/trademark and permitted channels, or request permission separately. Follow the client's brand requirements.
- **Review before publication.** Only move an actual, executed client's record to an announcement queue after checking the signed contract, current publicity preference, and authorized assets. Create a draft announcement; a human owner/operator must verify identity, wording, scope, logo rights, and confidentiality before publication. No automatic publishing purely from a signed intake event. Client preapproval of the exact draft may be a useful courtesy and may be required by a specific agreement; don't invent a universal requirement that defeats the default-in decision.
- **Healthcare confidentiality:** Never share patient information, PHI, psychotherapy/SUD information, nonpublic provider details, contracts/financials, claims, payer access, confidential operational information, or any other protected information. A business-practice name by itself is ordinarily not patient PHI, but confidentiality and trademark rights remain independent obligations. Do not present a factual welcome as a testimonial.
- **Later advocacy is separate.** Client testimonials, personal quotes, case studies, before/after revenue figures, performance claims, and referrals require distinct, specific authorization and factual substantiation, even if the practice did not opt out of a routine welcome.
- **Operational tracking (future implementation, not existing schema):** Keep contract_publicity_clause_version, signed_agreement_reference, publicity_status (included_by_contract / opted_out / pending_verification / withdrawn), notice_acknowledged_at, acknowledged_by, opt_out_received_at, opt_out_method, logo_use_authorized, channels_permitted, draft_url, internal_approved_at, posted_urls, takedown_requested_at. An opt-out must stop queued posts and trigger review/removal of existing posts where practical.
- **Marketing standard:** Simple welcoming copy about partnership and support, not fabricated client growth metrics or implied endorsement. When a client opts out, do not name, tag, photograph, or make them indirectly identifiable; generic company milestones can be used only without client-identifying cues.

For future AAP onboarding, contract, website, or Codex tasks, implement this **default-in / clear written opt-out** strategy rather than the previously proposed optional opt-in policy. Preserve the existing lightweight, EHR-agnostic onboarding and auditability. Do not claim this feature is live until verified.
